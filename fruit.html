🍎 Fruits

Fruits are an important part of a healthy diet. They are rich in vitamins, minerals, fiber, and antioxidants that help keep our body strong and healthy. Common fruits include apples, bananas, oranges, mangoes, grapes, and watermelons. Eating different kinds of fruits regularly can improve digestion, boost immunity, and provide natural energy. Fruits are also delicious and refreshing, making them a healthy choice for people of all ages.
